Discover its origin

From San Lorenzo, Ibiza

Our lemons grow on a farm in San Lorenzo, in the quiet green heart of Ibiza. They ripen slowly under the island sun — the island of the goddess Tanit, where every bottle begins.

Aged to perfection in Switzerland

In Switzerland, the peels are cut and prepared by hand and given time to rest until the limoncello reaches its golden balance — patient, precise and made with Swiss care.

26% vol.
Served ice cold

Golden, bright and smooth. Serve Can Limoncello well chilled, neat or over ice. Take a sip, close your eyes, and let Ibiza find you.
